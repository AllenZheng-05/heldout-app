# Loopline Bikes — planted usability problems (answer key)

16 planted problems (8 × S1, 8 × S2) and 3 traps (T). Severity: 1 cosmetic · 2 minor · 3 major · 4 blocker.

| ID | Problem | Screen | Element | How a person would notice | Stratum | Sev |
|----|---------|--------|---------|---------------------------|---------|-----|
| P01 | Search field has no label; only a placeholder ("Search bikes…") that disappears on typing. No `aria-label`/`<label>`. | Browse (`/`) | `search-input` | Screen reader announces an unnamed "search edit text"; DOM shows input with no accessible name. | S1 | 2 |
| P02 | Helper/hint text is very low contrast (#b8c0be on white, ≈1.9:1). | Dates, Rider, Review, Settings | `.hint`, e.g. "We'll send your receipt and pickup code here." / "This is a demo — no card is charged." | Hard to read; contrast checker fails WCAG 1.4.3. | S1 | 2 |
| P03 | Favourite (heart) icon button has no accessible name — SVG is `aria-hidden`, no label/text. | Browse | `fav-<bikeId>` e.g. `fav-volt-commuter` | Screen reader says just "toggle button"; DOM button has no name. | S1 | 2 |
| P04 | Focus indicator removed from every `.btn` (`.btn:focus, .btn:focus-visible { outline: none }`). Links/inputs keep theirs. | All screens | `rent-button`, `dates-continue`, `rider-continue`, `pay-button`, `settings-save`, dialog buttons | Tab through a form: focus "disappears" when it lands on a button. | S1 | 3 |
| P05 | Frame-size options are `<div onClick>` — not focusable, no role, no selected state exposed. Size is required to rent, so keyboard/AT users cannot rent any multi-size bike. | Bike detail (`/bikes/:id`) | `size-option-M` (etc.) inside `size-options` | Tab skips the sizes; pressing Rent gives "Please choose a frame size." with no way to choose. | S1 | 4 |
| P06 | Extras quantity −/+ buttons are 22×22 px (also on phones), close together. | Rider & extras (`/book/rider`) | `extra-helmet-plus`, `extra-lock-minus`, etc. | Hard to tap accurately on a phone; target-size check fails (< 24/44 px). | S1 | 2 |
| P07 | Rider-form validation only shows a generic top banner "Please fix the errors in the form." Invalid fields get a red border only — no per-field message, no `aria-invalid`/`aria-describedby`, banner isn't a live region. | Rider & extras | `rider-error-banner`; fields `rider-name`, `rider-email`, `rider-phone`, `rider-height` | Submit with a bad value: you're told something is wrong but not what or how to fix it; colour is the only cue. (Contrast: the Dates and Payment steps do per-field errors properly.) | S1 | 3 |
| P08 | "Cancel" means two opposite things: the list button "Cancel" (cancel rental) opens a dialog whose "Cancel" button keeps the rental and "OK" cancels it. | My rentals (`/rentals`) | `cancel-<id>` → `dialog-cancel` ("Cancel"), `dialog-ok` ("OK") | Users who click "Cancel" again in the dialog expecting to confirm don't cancel; or vice-versa. Labels don't say what they do. | S1 | 3 |
| P09 | On phones (≤640 px) the pay bar is `position: fixed` at the bottom and covers the terms checkbox, which is the last thing in the form; there is no bottom padding, so it can't be scrolled into view. Tapping Pay just says "Accept the rental terms to continue." | Review & pay (`/book/review`), mobile width | `terms-checkbox` / `terms-label` under `paybar` | At 375 px wide, scroll to bottom: checkbox is hidden behind the bar; checkout is effectively blocked. | S2 | 3 |
| P10 | "Save changes" saves silently — no toast, no message, no button state change. | Settings (`/settings`) | `settings-save` | Click Save: nothing visibly happens, users unsure it worked and click repeatedly or leave. | S2 | 2 |
| P11 | Payment has a ~1.8 s simulated round-trip with no loading state; the Pay button stays enabled and each click places another booking. | Review & pay | `pay-button` | Click Pay: nothing seems to happen for ~2 s; clicking again creates a duplicate rental (two entries in My rentals = double charge). | S2 | 3 |
| P12 | An "Autumn weekdays" promo banner is injected above the bike grid ~1.2 s after load, pushing all cards down (layout shift). | Browse | `promo-banner` | Content jumps while you're about to click a card; you may hit the wrong bike. | S2 | 2 |
| P13 | Business rule contradicts the offer: the hero says "Rent a bike by the hour or by the day" and every bike shows an hourly price, but the dates step requires the return *date* to be after the pickup date (same-day → "Return date must be after the pickup date.") and pricing is per day only. Hourly rental is impossible. | Browse hero → Dates (`/book/dates`) | `return-date`, `return-date-error`; hero h1; card prices "$X/hour" | Try to rent for an afternoon: you can't; minimum is a full day at the daily rate. | S2 | 3 |
| P14 | Misleading wording: "✓ Free cancellation" on the detail page and every checkout summary, but cancelling in My rentals charges a 50% fee. | Bike detail / checkout summary vs My rentals dialog | `free-cancel-badge`, summary "✓ Free cancellation" vs `cancel-dialog` text | Book on the strength of "free cancellation", then discover a 50% fee when you try to cancel. | S2 | 3 |
| P15 | Rider height must be 150–210 cm, but the shop rents the "Sprout 20″" kids' bike (riders 115–135 cm). A parent booking it for a child cannot proceed, and (with P07) only sees a red border with no reason. | Rider & extras for `sprout-20` | `rider-height` (min=150) | Book the kids' bike, enter the child's height (e.g. 125): blocked with no explanation. | S2 | 3 |
| P16 | Confirmation is a dead end: the checkout header hides the main nav and the logo is not a link, and the confirmation page has no link to My rentals, the booking, or home. | Confirmation (`/confirmation/:id`) | `confirmation`; `brand` (a `<span>`, not a link) | After paying there's nowhere to go; only the browser Back button (which returns to the payment page) or typing a URL. | S2 | 2 |
| T1 | "Skip to main content" link is positioned off-screen and only appears on keyboard focus. Intentional, correct pattern. | All screens | `skip-link` | Looks like hidden/off-screen content; it's a standard skip link that becomes visible on focus. | T | – |
| T2 | Bike card images have empty `alt=""`. Intentional: they are decorative illustrations; the bike's name is the adjacent heading link, so a text alt would just duplicate it. (The detail-page image has a descriptive alt.) | Browse | `.card-img` inside `bike-card-<id>` | Looks like "missing alt text"; it's correct for decorative images. | T | – |
| T3 | Deleting the account is a low-emphasis red text link, then requires typing DELETE; "Permanently delete" stays disabled until you do. Intentional friction for a destructive, irreversible action; there's a clear "Keep my account" exit. | Settings | `delete-account`, `delete-confirm-input`, `delete-confirm-button` | Looks like a hidden/disabled control or an obstacle; it's deliberate safeguarding. | T | – |

Notes
- S1 = detectable from DOM/CSS structure; S2 = needs judgment about the app's purpose or watching it run.
- Judgment-about-purpose items: P13, P14, P15, P16 (also P11).
- Everything else in the app is intended to work correctly (per-field errors on Dates and Payment steps, labelled selects, 44 px buttons, dialog with Esc-to-close and initial focus, card number accepts spaces, booking draft persists when going back).
